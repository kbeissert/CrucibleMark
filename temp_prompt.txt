=== ANFORDERUNG A: SECURITY (Non-Negotiable) ===
- 'Zero Knowledge' End-to-End Encryption (E2EE).

=== ANFORDERUNG B: FEATURE (Must-Have) ===

- Kein schwerer Client-Side Index (Browser/Mobile Only, kein lokaler Storage).
- Keine Homomorphe Verschlüsselung (zu langsam / zu hoher Overhead).
