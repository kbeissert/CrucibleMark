### 6. **Critical Gaps in Model Response:**

1. **Analysis lacks structured reasoning:** The model lists missing elements but doesn't explain *why* each matters or what the *impact* is. The golden standard's table format with "Impact" column is more diagnostic.

2. **Hook is statistic-based, not scenario-based:** The model opens with "Falls dein Passwort jemals geleakt wird – und das passiert alle 3 Sekunden" (statistic). The golden standard opens with "Stell dir vor — du wachst morgens auf. Dein E-Mail-Account ist weg" (scenario). The golden standard explicitly justifies this: "activates loss-aversion, the strongest motivator."

3. **Pattern Interrupt advice is less critical:** The model's pattern interrupt focuses on SMS vs. App security. The golden standard's focuses on backup codes, which is more critical for account recovery.

4. **CTA is command-based, not achievement-based:** The model says "Like wenn du sicherer bist" (command). The golden standard says "Die meisten tun das nie – du hast es in 5 Minuten erledigt" (achievement framing). The latter is psychologically more effective.

5. **Easter Egg is transactional, not relational:** The model offers a discount code. The golden standard encourages community engagement with a keyword. The latter better serves the task requirement to "strengthen community engagement."

6. **Fewer [PAUSE] markers:** The model uses fewer explicit pauses, relying on punctuation. The golden standard uses 15+ [PAUSE] markers, which is more precise for video production.

7. **Less granular screen annotations:** The model's annotations are functional but less sequential. The golden standard breaks each action into separate lines, making it easier for an editor to execute.
